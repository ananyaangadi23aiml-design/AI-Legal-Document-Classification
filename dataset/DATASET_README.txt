Legal Document Training Dataset - 125 PDFs
============================================
5 categories x 25 PDFs each.

Categories: affidavit, agreement, contract, court_judgement, legal_notice.
The PDFs are structured, synthetic training documents created for this project,
using legal-document structure and terminology informed by public legal-document
resources. They are not copies of court records or private legal documents.

Recommended external references:
- VidhikDastaavej (L-NLProc, Hugging Face), MIT licensed legal-document dataset.
- eCourts Judgments and Orders portal, Government of India.

Use these files as a balanced pipeline/training dataset. For a publication-grade
evaluation, add a separate held-out set of real documents and report its metrics.
